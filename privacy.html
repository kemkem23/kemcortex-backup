Privacy policy - kemcortex backup

Last updated: 8 October 2026

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the Google Drive API to upload backup files to the owner's own Google Drive, list them, and check their size.

What the app does with it

Nothing else. The app does not collect, sell or share any Google user data. Nothing is sent to third parties. There is no analytics or tracking.

Who uses it

Only its owner. The app is not available to anyone else.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the GitHub profile of the owner of this site.
